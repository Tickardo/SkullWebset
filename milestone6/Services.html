Services Offered

I offers a range of property valuation services tailored to different property types. Single-family home appraisals are available for $375, while condo appraisals are priced at $450. More complex properties, including luxury homes, complexes, and multi-unit properties, are also supported, with fees ranging from $700 to $800 depending on size and complexity. In addition, the company provides rental survey services for $200, helping clients determine fair market rental values. Overall, the company delivers flexible appraisal options for homeowners, investors, and real estate professionals seeking accurate and reliable property valuations.
